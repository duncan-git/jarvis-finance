Privacy policy

JARVIS Finance is a private application used by one person, its owner. It is not a public service.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the Gmail "send" permission to email the owner's own address with market and portfolio summaries. It does not read, store, copy or share any other email content.

What it stores

The login token is stored on the owner's own devices. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The owner of this application, via the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
